What makes this AI different?

01 / EXPERIENCE

200K games of self-play

Separate X and O agents played against each other, gathering experience from victories, losses, and draws.

02 / REWARDS

Learned, not programmed

Win = +1, loss = −1, draw = 0. Q-learning updates action values using discounted future rewards.

03 / POLICY

ε-greedy decisions

Casual and Balanced sometimes explore randomly; Focused always exploits the highest learned Q-value. No minimax.
